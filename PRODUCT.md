# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Households managing a shared family budget. Two or more people with joint visibility into income, expenses, recurring commitments, and upcoming obligations. They're not financial analysts — they want clarity on where they stand without having to dig for it. Primary workflow: check balances, log a transaction, review what's scheduled. They use it regularly, not obsessively.

## Product Purpose

Aliran is a shared household finance tracker. It exists to give families a single quiet place to see their financial picture: what came in, what went out, what's committed, what's due. Success looks like a household that doesn't get surprised by their finances — not because they've optimized aggressively, but because they can see clearly.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, focused, quiet. Aliran gets out of the way. It doesn't celebrate your spending or panic about it. It just shows you what's true, without noise.

## Anti-references

- **Mint / YNAB** — widget-heavy dashboards where everything competes for attention. Aliran never crowds the screen.
- **Robinhood / crypto apps** — dark-mode hype, neon accents, gamified UI. Finance is not entertainment here.
- **Generic SaaS** — sidebar + identical card grids. No default layouts, no template thinking.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't explain.** The data speaks. Labels are for orientation, not instruction.
2. **One primary thing per screen.** Never two competing focal points. If the eye has to choose, the layout has failed.
3. **Quiet confidence.** Information density is fine; visual noise is not. The app should feel like a well-kept ledger, not a dashboard.
4. **Shared, not siloed.** The UI should feel natural to glance at together — at the kitchen table, on a phone, between two people.
5. **No drama.** Negative numbers, overdue commitments, zero balances — render them clearly, without alarm. The user brings the emotion; the app brings the facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Sufficient color contrast for all text. Semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable flows. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Color is never the sole carrier of meaning — income/expense/scheduled/receivable are also distinguished by label and position.
